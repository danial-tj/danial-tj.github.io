# Vancouver backdrop correction

User correction: remove the added foreground trees; represent North Vancouver, West Vancouver, Stanley Park and downtown in their correct relationships.

The image uses an elevated offshore viewpoint west of First Narrows, looking east/east-southeast into Burrard Inlet. This is an inferred composition from the mapped geography, not a traced photograph.

- Left/north shore: West Vancouver in the nearer west, North Vancouver farther east; mountains rise inland behind that shore.
- Right/south shore: Stanley Park at the nearer northwestern end of the downtown peninsula, visibly connected to the West End/downtown farther right. No water channel separates the park from downtown.
- Lions Gate Bridge: crosses First Narrows from North Shore to the park's northern end at Prospect Point. Its south approach continues through the park.
- Burrard Inlet separates the northern shore from the Stanley Park/downtown side. Coal Harbour is a sheltered indentation, not a strait cutting the peninsula in two.
- Mountain skyline, buildings and scale remain drawn approximations. Avoid claiming photographic or survey accuracy.
